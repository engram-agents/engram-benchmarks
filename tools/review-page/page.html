How the scores and statuses work

Each question has rubric lines from the dataset. A line scores 1 if the answer meets it, 0.5 if partly, 0 if not; the question scores the mean of its lines, and the run scores the mean over its questions.

The official score uses the dataset's answers exactly as given. The adjusted score re-rates a question against a corrected answer, and only when that question is tied to a dataset problem already checked in the raw data or further along.

A possible problem climbs four steps: suspected, checked in the raw data, confirmed by a second reader who had not seen our verdict, ready to report. A string search never proves something is missing from the conversation: that needs a full read of every exchange about it, user and assistant lines both.

BEAM's own published way of combining rubric marks has not yet been checked against this one.
