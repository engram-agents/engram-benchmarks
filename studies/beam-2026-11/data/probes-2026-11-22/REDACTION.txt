CLASSES
A  An internal team-message number becomes the UTC date and minute of that message's own timestamp, truncated
   (2026-11-22T19:58Z). The sender and the item, where the original cited them, stay as written beside it. Where two
   cited messages joined by a slash share a minute, both carry seconds (2026-11-22T20:23:57Z/2026-11-22T20:23:59Z).
   29 distinct messages: 26 timestamps read by the study lead, 3 by the design owner (the security seat's messages
   of 2026-11-22T19:29Z and two of 2026-11-22T20:02Z, not open to the study lead).
B  A host path becomes its role: <probe base>, <probe base slug>, <pinned claude-2.1.328 binary>, <study lead's
   private probe folder>, <the host's shared agent directory>.
C  Account, uid and file-mode wording becomes role wording: 'readable by the study lead only' where it states an
   access boundary; 'the study lead's account'; the temp path that carries the numeric uid becomes
   <Claude Code's per-uid temp directory> (the repository's privacy gate matches that path's SHAPE, not only the
   number, so a placeholder for the number alone was not enough; first found by that gate); a seat's own
   working-session id becomes 'the study lead's interactive session'. Claude Code's own 'per-uid temp tree' layout
   names nobody and stays. The study's own harness session ids stay.
D  Which service runs on the host and on what port: the service's name becomes 'a host service', the port <port>.
Seat names stay where they attribute a read or a ruling. Nothing else differs.
